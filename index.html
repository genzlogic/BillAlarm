The problem

Bills are easy to forget. Late fees are not.

Every month the same bills come back, each from a different company, on a different date, through a different SMS, email or app. Miss one and it costs you.

Scattered everywhere

Card statements, electricity bills and recharge reminders arrive in different places, so there's no single view of what you owe.

Different due dates

Each bill has its own cycle. Keeping track of who is due when, in your head, is how dates slip by.

Missing one costs money

A late payment can mean late fees, interest on your card, or a service cut-off, and late card payments can hurt your credit score.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which bills can I track?

Credit card, electricity, internet and mobile bills. You can add as many as you need, and set any of them to repeat every month.

When will I be reminded?

Reminders start 8 days before a bill's due date, with notifications at 9 AM and 6 PM each day. Make sure notifications are allowed for Bill Alarm in your iPhone Settings.

Does Bill Alarm pay my bills?

No. Bill Alarm reminds you and helps you keep track. You pay through your bank, card or biller as usual, then mark the bill as paid in the app.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Do you see my card number?

No. Bill Alarm only asks for the last 4 digits so you can tell your cards apart, and that stays on your device.
